About Us

Spaces shaped around you

At Lumen & Archue, we transform spaces into meaningful experiences. Every project is custom made around you, never copied from a template. We blend creativity, functionality and timeless aesthetics to create spaces inside and out, that reflect your lifestyle and personality.

From the first sketch to the final finish, one dedicated team designs, builds and delivers, so the space you imagine is the space you walk into. We proudly serve homes and workplaces across Tamil Nadu, Kerala and Karnataka.

I

Made Around You

Every layout, unit and finish is drawn around how you live, never copied from a template.

II

Light as Material

Spotlights, cove lines and backlit details are planned with the design from the start, so rooms feel warm day and night.

III

Detail in the Making

Joinery, panelling and metalwork are built by our own team, with every junction and finish checked.

Our process

Consultation

We meet to understand your lifestyle, needs, taste and budget, and visit the site to take measurements.

Quotation

You receive a clear, upfront quote for your project.

Commencement

A small advance confirms your project and begins the design work.

Design and Approval

We present your custom design, and nothing begins on site until you approve it.

Execution

Our team builds to a fixed timeline with regular updates, and our inspectors check every stage on site.

Handover

After final quality checks, we hand over a space that is ready to live in.
